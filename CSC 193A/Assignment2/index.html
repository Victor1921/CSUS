Music is great to listen to and can elevate your mood, but did you know it can do a lot more as well? Music can actually reduce stress, anxiety, and help with focus and memory. Background music (music without lyrics) especially has been known to help with attention. While music has many individual benefits it can also help you create new relationships with others with shared intrests. Many who share the same taste in music develop very strong and long lasting friendships.
